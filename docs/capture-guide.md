# Capture guide: how to film a room

> Draft (Step 0). Based on the PRD and common Gaussian Splatting practice; to be
> updated with what actually works in our tests (Step 7).

- **Duration:** 1–3 minutes per room.
- **Move slowly and smoothly.** Fast motion = blurry frames = failed reconstruction.
- **Walk around the room**, don't just turn on the spot. Cover the room from several positions.
- **Film at 2–3 heights** (e.g. chest, head, and low near furniture).
- **Good, even lighting:** turn lights on, avoid strong backlight from windows.
- **Avoid mirrors, windows and glossy surfaces** as much as possible; they confuse pose estimation.
- **Landscape or portrait:** to be decided by our tests.
- **Resolution:** 1080p or higher.
